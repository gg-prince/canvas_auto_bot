Follow the steps below. If you prefer, watch the video tutorial.

Written guide

  1. 1
    Open Canvas

    Log in to your school's Canvas account using a browser.

  2. 2
    Open Calendar

    Go to Calendar. Find the calendar feed option (usually near the bottom of the calendar sidebar).

  3. 3
    Copy the calendar feed link

    Choose the calendar feed link and copy its URL. It should be a calendar/iCalendar feed—not your password or a login link.

  4. 4
    Paste it below

    Submit the link. We'll check that it can be read before saving it.
